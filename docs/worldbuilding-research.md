## 2. Region themes

Tier key: T1 levels 1-4 (CR 1/8-4), T2 5-10 (CR 3-10), T3 11-16 (CR 9-17), T4 17-20 (CR 15+). A region holds
sites of several tiers; distance from town and depth into the region raise the tier.

### 2.1 Greenmarch (river farmland and copse)
- Terrain: rolling fields, hedgerows, mills, river fords. Mood: pastoral, quietly threatened.
- Tags: grassland, forest, hills, ruins.
- Allies: human hamlets, halfling shires (food, pipeweed, scouts). Neutrals: travelling tinkers, a river ferry clan.
- Enemies: T1 bandits, goblin raiders, giant rats, wolves; T2 gnoll war band, ogre, wererat in the mill;
  T3 a green dragon nesting in the old wood; T4 none native (spillover only).
- Sites: barrow, bandit camp, flooded mill, watchtower ruin. Names: "the Crooked Mill", "Hollowfield Barrow",
  "Gallows Ford", "the Old Tithe Tower".
- Alliance gives: cheap food and ale, militia levies, halfling scouts (stealthy rogue recruits), road safety.

### 2.2 Thornwood (old deep forest)
- Terrain: giant trees, bramble walls, mossy stone circles. Mood: ancient, watchful.
- Tags: forest, swamp, feywild, ruins.
- Allies: elf enclave (bows, healing herbs, rangers). Neutrals: druid circle, treants, a fey court that trades
  in favours, woodcutter hermits.
- Enemies: T1 wolves, giant spiders, twig blights; T2 owlbear, dryad gone wrong, a hag coven's servants;
  T3 the hag coven, a corrupted treant; T4 an archfey hunt riding through.
- Sites: hag hut, spider hollow, stone circle, sunken shrine. Names: "Webbed Hollow", "the Weeping Ring",
  "Grandmother Tallow's Hut", "the Briar Gate".
- Alliance gives: rare herbs and potions, elf archers, ranger and druid heroes, knowledge of fey bargains.

### 2.3 Ironcrag Hills and Highpeaks (mountain holds)
- Terrain: scree, pine ridges, high passes, mine adits. Mood: hard, proud, under siege.
- Tags: hills, mountain, caves, underworld.
- Allies: dwarf hold (steel, masons, heavy infantry), gnome burrow (tinkers, illusions). Neutrals: hill giants
  who can be paid, stone-giant monks, goat-herding clans.
- Enemies: T1 kobolds, goblins; T2 orc horde, hobgoblin legion, griffons, trolls; T3 a red dragon in the
  sealed deep, fire giant forge; T4 an ancient dragon or a primordial in the roots of the mountain.
- Sites: lost dwarf delving, orc fortress, dragon lair, giant steading. Names: "Hammerdeep (fallen)",
  "the Red Stair", "Skullpass Keep", "the Anvil That Burned".
- Alliance gives: ore and arms, stonework (cheaper walls), shield-wall troops, fighter and cleric heroes,
  maps of the deep roads.

### 2.4 Mirefen (black marsh and delta)
- Terrain: reed beds, stilt villages, sinking ruins, fog. Mood: sticky, rotting, uncanny.
- Tags: swamp, coast, lake, ruins, temple.
- Allies: lizardfolk tribe (tough guides, trade by barter, wary of humans). Neutrals: bullywug fiefdom that
  will deal, a marsh witch, eel fishers.
- Enemies: T1 stirges, giant frogs, will-o'-wisps; T2 black dragon wyrmling, troll, swamp hag; T3 an adult
  black dragon, a drowned-god cult; T4 an aboleth-like intelligence below the silt.
- Sites: drowned temple, sunken city, hag's island, leech pits. Names: "the Sinking Saints",
  "Lanternless Isle", "the Gullet", "Old Coralwick Under the Reeds".
- Alliance gives: poisons and antitoxins, marsh guides (faster travel), lizardfolk warriors, rare reagents.

### 2.5 Duneglass Waste (desert and badlands)
- Terrain: dunes, salt flats, mesas, buried cities. Mood: scorched, timeless, mirage-haunted.
- Tags: desert, badlands, tomb, ruins, temple.
- Allies: oasis free city (caravans, spices). Neutrals: nomad clans, sphinx guardians, djinn-blooded traders.
- Enemies: T1 giant scorpions, jackals, dust mephits; T2 gnolls, mummies, a blue dragon wyrmling;
  T3 mummy lord, blue dragon, lamia; T4 a god-king waking in his pyramid.
- Sites: tomb-pyramid, glass-sand crater, caravan ruin, oracle shrine. Names: "the Sleeping King's Stair",
  "Saltbone Crossing", "the Mirror Pan", "Tomb of the Nine Masks".
- Alliance gives: spices, silk and camels (trade income), caravan routes, desert lore, monk and warlock heroes.

### 2.6 Rimefell (arctic tundra and glacier)
- Terrain: ice fields, frozen lakes, aurora, whale-bone camps. Mood: stark, lonely, dangerous beauty.
- Tags: arctic, mountain, coast, caves.
- Allies: reindeer-herding folk, a hall of frost-sworn warriors. Neutrals: frost giants who honour duels,
  yeti, ice-fishing hermits.
- Enemies: T1 wolves, ice mephits; T2 winter wolves, yeti, ogres; T3 frost giant jarl, white dragon, remorhaz;
  T4 a frozen titan or the queen of winter fey.
- Sites: ice cave, frozen ship, giant's hall, rime-buried barrow. Names: "the Iceforged Hall", "Wreck of the
  Pale Gull", "Howling Shelf", "the Frostwight Mound".
- Alliance gives: furs, cold-weather gear, barbarian heroes, beasts of burden, sagas (lore on giants).

### 2.7 Saltreach (coast and isles)
- Terrain: cliffs, coves, reefs, lighthouses, wrecks. Mood: salty, piratical, storm-tossed.
- Tags: coast, ocean, caves, urban.
- Allies: fishing town and a port free city. Neutrals: merchant guild, merfolk, smugglers.
- Enemies: T1 pirates, crabs, sahuagin scouts; T2 sea hag, harpies, sahuagin raiding party; T3 dragon turtle,
  pirate lich-captain; T4 a kraken-class leviathan.
- Sites: smugglers' cave, wrecker's lighthouse, drowned keep, reef temple. Names: "the False Light",
  "Gull's Tooth Cove", "Brinehold", "the Barnacled Chapel".
- Alliance gives: shipping (trade multiplier), salt fish, sailors, swashbucklers and bards, maps of distant shores.

### 2.8 Ashen Barrows (haunted moor and old battlefield)
- Terrain: heath, standing stones, mass graves, broken keeps. Mood: grey, mournful, restless dead.
- Tags: grassland, hills, tomb, ruins, shadowfell.
- Allies: a monastery or temple order (healing, clerics). Neutrals: gravekeepers, ghost of a fallen king who
  wants a debt paid.
- Enemies: T1 skeletons, zombies, ghouls; T2 wights, wraith, necromancer; T3 death knight, vampire lord;
  T4 a lich and its phylactery-vault.
- Sites: barrow mound, necropolis, battlefield ossuary, fallen chapel. Names: "the Field of Spears",
  "King-under-Turf", "the Lantern Ossuary", "Chapel of Unrung Bells".
- Alliance gives: holy water and healing, cleric and paladin heroes, protection from undead raids, burial lore.

### 2.9 The Deep Roads (underworld)
- Terrain: fungus forests, chasms, underground seas, lightless cities. Mood: alien, claustrophobic.
- Tags: underworld, caves, ruins, temple.
- Allies: a dwarf outpost below, a deep-gnome enclave. Neutrals: myconid circle, a merchant of the dark.
- Enemies: T1 kobolds, giant centipedes, grey ooze; T2 hook horrors, gricks, duergar-like raiders (our own),
  minotaur; T3 mind-flayer-type psion (original, not the WotC IP monster), purple worm, beholder-style eye tyrant
  (original); T4 a demon prince's gate.
- Sites: fungal cavern, underground lake, slaver city, sealed vault. Names: "the Glimmering Rot",
  "Lake Without Shore", "Chainmarket", "the Vault That Breathes".
- Alliance gives: rare minerals and mushrooms, darkvision guides, deep maps (unlocks deeper sites).
  Note: beholder, mind flayer, githyanki, etc. are not in the SRD; use originals only.

### 2.10 Shattered Kingdom (ruined empire and wasteland of magic)
- Terrain: broken highways, toppled colossi, wild-magic scars. Mood: faded glory, dangerous curiosity.
- Tags: ruins, urban, grassland, badlands, temple.
- Allies: a free city of scholars, survivors' enclaves. Neutrals: a mage guild, a golem still keeping its oath.
- Enemies: T1 bandits, animated armour; T2 cultists, gargoyles, a rogue mage; T3 a cult's demon, iron golem,
  rakshasa; T4 an imprisoned archmage or a pit fiend summoned through the old gate.
- Sites: sunken library, colossus head, wizard tower, imperial vault. Names: "the Drowned Athenaeum",
  "Crown of the Fallen Colossus", "the Tower of Inverted Stairs", "Vault of the Last Emperor".
- Alliance gives: spell scrolls, magic item crafting, wizard heroes, ancient knowledge (research boost).

### 2.11 Cinderwastes (volcanic)
- Terrain: lava fields, obsidian, sulphur springs, ash plains. Mood: violent, primordial.
- Tags: mountain, badlands, caves.
- Allies: a salamander-tamer clan or a fire-forging dwarf exile hold. Neutrals: azer smiths, fire giant traders.
- Enemies: T1 magma mephits, fire beetles; T2 salamanders, hell hounds; T3 fire giants, red dragon;
  T4 a fire elemental lord's breach.
- Sites: forge-temple, lava tube, obsidian spire, cooling caldera. Names: "the Ember Choir", "Glasswound",
  "Smith-King's Furnace", "the Ashfall Gate".
- Alliance gives: masterwork arms, fire-resistant gear, sorcerer heroes.

### 2.12 Feywild Fringe (otherworld crossing, late game)
- Terrain: twilight glades, mirrored lakes, seasons out of order. Mood: beautiful, capricious, unsafe.
- Tags: feywild, forest, lake.
- Allies: a summer-court host (rare, by bargain). Neutrals: sprites, satyrs, pixies. Enemies: T2 redcaps,
  quicklings; T3 night hag, green dragon; T4 the winter court's queen.
- Sites: fairy ring, hollow hill, mirror lake, thorn palace. Names: "the Feast That Never Ends",
  "Moonbridge", "Under-the-Hill", "Palace of Unkept Promises".
- Alliance gives: blessings (morale, luck), fey-touched heroes, but every gift carries a debt.

## 3. Faction archetypes

| Archetype | Default attitude | Shifts up with | Shifts down with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human village / hamlet | Friendly | Cleared nearby threats, fair trade, shelter in hard winters | Taxes, raids not answered, heroes behaving badly |
| Dwarf hold | Wary | Fulfilled contracts, returning lost relics, killing their old enemies (orcs, dragons) | Broken oaths, late payment, looting dwarf tombs |
| Elf enclave | Aloof | Protecting the forest, fey lore, gifts of art or song | Logging, fire, killing sacred beasts |
| Halfling shire | Friendly | Good food trade, safe roads | Danger brought to their door |
| Gnome burrow | Curious | Rare materials, inventions, puzzles solved | Boredom, destroying machines |
| Free city / merchant guild | Neutral (profit) | Trade volume, safe caravans, exclusive contracts | Tariffs, banditry on routes, competing guild |
| Druid circle | Neutral | Restoring corrupted land, slaying aberrations/undead | Overhunting, mining sacred sites |
| Hermit / sage | Neutral | Rare books, quiet, a specific errand | Crowds, pestering |
| Giants | Neutral to hostile | Honour (duels, feasts, gifts of scale), respect of their lands | Trespass, theft, cowardice |
| Fey court | Capricious | Bargains kept to the letter, gifts of names or memories | Iron, broken bargains, rudeness |
| Nomad clans | Neutral | Water and pasture rights, hospitality returned | Fences, stolen herds |
| Lizardfolk tribe | Neutral (pragmatic) | Meat, tools, help against a shared predator | Draining the marsh |
| Goblinoid tribe | Hostile (some bribable) | Paying tribute, beating their boss (they follow strength) | Weakness, a new warlord |
| Orc horde | Hostile | Very rarely a truce after a defeated warchief | Any expansion into their land |
| Undead / necromancer | Hostile | Never; only removed | Grave-robbing raises more |
| Cult | Hostile, hidden | Never; infiltration rises with despair (low morale, plague) | Exposure, clerics, temples |
| Dragon | Hostile or extortionate | Tribute (may become a "neutral" racket), slaying rivals | Theft from hoard, insult |
| Bandit lord | Hostile but pragmatic | Bribes, pardons (can turn into a militia), crushing defeat | Bounties, hangings |
| Hag coven | Hostile with bargains | Deals (always costly) | Breaking a deal, killing a sister |

Mechanic sketch: each faction has an attitude score (-100..100) in bands Hostile / Wary / Neutral / Friendly /
Allied. Daily tick drifts it toward its default. Events push it: quest completed for them (+), threat in their
region
cleared (+), gift (+, diminishing), trade volume (+ small daily), insult/incident caused by heroes (-), ignoring
their request for help (-). Allied unlocks goods, troops, a hero class recruit or knowledge (site reveals).

## 4. Design takeaways

Autonomous parties choosing quests (MMORPG Tycoon 2 style)
- Each site carries a tier and a recommended level band; parties score quests by expected reward x success
  chance - travel cost, where success chance comes from party level vs site tier (cheap lookup, no sim).
- Hard gate plus soft pull: parties cannot pick a site more than one tier above them (gate), and gain a bonus for
  sites at their level (pull). Tycoon players found zones need a level gate or heroes outlevel the content.
- Too much T1 content near town makes heroes outlevel the next ring; balance count of sites per tier per ring,
  and let cleared T1 sites respawn slowly rather than instantly.
- Quest givers point outward: a T1 village quest can chain to a T2 site deeper in the same region ("the
  goblins were fleeing something"). This draws parties to the next band naturally.
- Monsters keep parties, scenery draws them: unexplored regions get a curiosity bonus for scouting parties,
  but parties settle into regions with dense level-appropriate encounters.
- Personality bends the score: reckless heroes accept one tier up, cautious ones only at-level; greed weights
  loot, piety weights undead/cult sites, rangers prefer wilderness.
- Keep it deterministic: score, then sort by score then site id, then take from `Town.Rng` only for ties
  by design.

Points-of-light settlement pockets
- The town is one light; ally settlements are others. Between them, roads are dangerous and knowledge fades:
  far sites start as rumours (unknown tier, vague name) until scouted or a friendly faction shares maps.
- Every allied pocket extends safety: a radius where wandering threats are weaker and travel is cheaper.
  Losing an ally (raided, or attitude drop) dims that light and lets enemy tiers creep inward.
- Region danger is a gradient: ring 1 (T1) around lights, ring 2 (T2) in the wilds, ring 3 (T3) in the
  heart of each region, T4 sites are unique world-threats found only by following quest chains.
- Enemy factions grow if ignored (goblin camp to warband to horde; cult to demon summoning), which gives
  autonomous parties urgency and the player a reason to post bounties.
- Each region should have one ally, one neutral with a price and two to three enemy lines across tiers;
  that is enough texture for a tycoon pace without becoming a full RPG setting.
