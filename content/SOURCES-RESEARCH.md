# Where we looked for open D&D material

Rule (Conner, 2026-10-17): only CC-BY-4.0, CC0 or public-domain material is imported, with credit. Everything else,
which covers most homebrew, is inspiration only, and anything inspired by it is written fresh as a Crown & Compass original.

| Source | Licence | What we did |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SRD 5.2 and 5.1 (Wizards of the Coast) | CC-BY-4.0 | Imported (`content/srd`). |
| Black Flag SRD, Level Up A5E (Adventurer's Guide, Dungeon Delver's Guide, Gate Pass Gazette), Spells That Don't Suck | CC-BY-4.0 | Imported via Open5e (`content/open`). |
| Ironsworn and Ironsworn: Delve (Shawn Tomkin), via Datasworn | Mixed: oracles, delve sites and assets are CC-BY-4.0; foes, atlas, truths and premade sites are CC-BY-NC-SA | Imported only the CC-BY objects, checked per object: random tables, site themes and domains, talents. |
| Ironsworn: Starforged | CC-BY-4.0 | Skipped for now: science fiction. Its name and event tables could be mined later. |
| Kobold Press tomes, A5E Monstrous Menagerie, Tal'Dorei and other Open5e books | OGL-1.0a | Dropped by decision; replaced with originals. |
| Kristin Saint-Clair's Little Book of Beasts (itch.io, E. A. "taichara" Bisson) | Critter stats CC-BY-4.0; art and the in-world letter excluded | Imported from the critter-stats text file (49 creatures), converted from rules-light HD and armour to 5e numbers (`content/open/little-book-of-beasts`). |
| Khyberia SRD by Nick Stefanski (itch.io) | CC-BY-4.0 | Imported: 21 stat blocks (frogoblins, hedron constructs, koaxian b'lots, muck mephit, war snail, water guardian, wodyanoi) with the author's attribution wording. |
| The Midnight Spell Compendium by Eve Parker, Nocturnal Designs (itch.io; formerly JayPea's Spell Compendium) | CC-BY-4.0 for rules and spell text; Artificer references are under the Fan Content Policy | Imported: 99 new spells (one duplicated an SRD spell). Artificer dropped from class lists; the book's own priest class maps to cleric. |
| D&D Homebrew Wiki (dnd-wiki.org) | CC-BY-SA-3.0 | Share-alike would bind our data, so inspiration only. |
| Homebrewery, GM Binder, D&D Beyond homebrew, r/UnearthedArcana, most itch.io packs | No blanket licence; each author keeps all rights | Inspiration only. |
| World folklore and medieval bestiaries | Public domain | Inspiration for `inspiredBy` originals (folklore monsters and household charms), written fresh. |
